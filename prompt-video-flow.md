# Vídeo 3D do topo do site: prompts para o Google Flow

Feito a partir da identidade da Xavier Odontologia: fundo azul `#3567b8`, azul-marinho `#0f1d45`,
o dente facetado dourado do logo e um toque lima `#d9f24a`.

## Como gerar

1. No Flow, escolha **Text to Video**, proporção **16:9** e a melhor qualidade disponível.
2. Cole o **Prompt principal**. Gere 2 a 4 versões e fique com a que tiver o movimento mais suave
   e o fundo azul mais uniforme.
3. Baixe em MP4 e salve como `video/hero.mp4` dentro da pasta do site.
   - Se quiser uma imagem de capa, salve um quadro do vídeo como `video/hero.jpg` (opcional).
4. Abra o site. O vídeo entra sozinho no lugar da foto do topo.

O vídeo aparece cortado nas laterais no computador (o espaço é mais alto que largo),
por isso o dente precisa ficar sempre **no centro**. O prompt já pede isso.

## Prompt principal (loop do topo)

```
Premium 3D animated loop for a dental clinic website. A single elegant stylized tooth,
designed as a faceted geometric gem like a low-poly crystal: smooth pearl-white faces
outlined by thin polished gold edges, catching soft highlights as it turns. The tooth floats
perfectly centered in frame and rotates very slowly on its vertical axis, with a gentle
up-and-down hover.

Background: a solid, even cobalt blue (#3567b8), matte and clean, with a very soft darker
navy vignette at the edges. A few tiny warm golden light particles and one or two small
soft lime-yellow glints drift slowly around the tooth. Subtle soft glow behind the tooth.

Lighting: soft studio key light from upper left, warm gold rim light, gentle reflections on
the gold edges, no harsh shadows. Camera: locked, centered, very slow push-in, shallow
depth of field. Keep the tooth in the central third of the frame with empty blue space on
the left and right sides.

Style: high-end 3D product render, minimal, calm, trustworthy, modern, luxurious but
friendly. 16:9, 4K detail, smooth seamless loop, the last frame matches the first.
No text, no letters, no logos, no people, no faces, no gums, no blood, no dental tools,
no busy background.
```

### Se precisar ajustar

| Problema | Acrescente ao final do prompt |
|---|---|
| Dente parece realista demais / raiz feia | `stylized, simplified tooth shape, rounded crown, short smooth roots` |
| Fundo saiu com gradiente ou cor diferente | `flat uniform cobalt blue background, no gradients, no patterns` |
| Movimento rápido ou câmera mexendo muito | `extremely slow and calm motion, almost still` |
| Apareceu texto ou símbolo | `absolutely no text or symbols anywhere` |
| Dente saindo do centro | `tooth stays exactly centered the entire time` |

## Cenas extras (vídeo institucional, Instagram ou outra seção)

Cada cena dá cerca de 8 segundos. Junte no editor de cenas do Flow.
Para Reels/Stories, troque `16:9` por `9:16`.

**1. Abertura: o dente se forma**
```
3D animation: thin polished gold lines draw themselves in the air and assemble into a
faceted geometric tooth, then pearl-white faces fill in between the gold edges.
Solid cobalt blue (#3567b8) background, soft warm golden particles, slow centered camera,
premium minimal 3D render, 16:9, no text, no logos.
```

**2. Cuidado e proteção**
```
3D animation: a faceted pearl-white tooth with gold edges floats on a cobalt blue
background; a soft transparent glowing shield gently forms around it, with delicate
lime-yellow light ripples passing across the shield. Calm, reassuring, slow orbit camera,
premium 3D render, 16:9, no text.
```

**3. Tecnologia e clínica moderna**
```
3D animation: a thin soft light scan line sweeps slowly across a faceted white-and-gold
tooth, revealing a delicate gold wireframe that fades back into the smooth pearl surface.
Cobalt blue background with a dark navy vignette, modern dental technology feel, friendly
not cold, slow camera, high-end 3D render, 16:9, no text.
```

**4. Encerramento: sorriso**
```
3D animation: several faceted pearl-white teeth with thin gold edges glide softly into place,
forming a gentle stylized smile arc in the center of the frame, warm golden glow spreading
softly across a cobalt blue background, camera slowly pulls back, joyful and welcoming,
premium 3D render, 16:9, no text, no faces, no lips.
```

## Cuidados (código de ética do CFO)

- Não use o vídeo para prometer resultado ("sorriso perfeito garantido") nem mostrar antes/depois.
- Não coloque preço ou promoção no vídeo.
- Nome e CRO da Dra. Vilma continuam visíveis no site, no cartão sobre o vídeo.
